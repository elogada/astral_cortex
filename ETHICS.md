AstraMech and the astral_cortex framework are developed with the primary intent of advancing accessible, educational, and locally reproducible embodied AI systems. The project emphasizes human-in-the-loop interaction, local or edge-first computation, and transparent system behavior to support learning, assistive use-cases, and civic or resilience-oriented applications. This framework is not designed for autonomous weapons control, kinetic targeting, or mass-surveillance deployment, and such use-cases are considered outside the project’s scope and intent. As an open-source system released under the MIT License, downstream use cannot be fully controlled; however, contributors and adopters are encouraged to align implementations with principles of accessibility, proportionality, and respect for human agency. The project intentionally favors modularity and reproducibility on commodity hardware to enable experimentation, education, and capacity-building—particularly in resource-constrained environments.
